## Public exposure and stop conditions

The public link can be shared beyond the group. The Apps Script endpoint already accepts anonymous requests. A person who can select a valid group can obtain a scorer session, see that group's scores and history, and change or finish the group before lock. A session identifies an operation and group, with no proof of personal identity. Unsolicited submissions or quota usage can interrupt play. Public GET routes can expose player names and handicaps, groups and published results. Public standings withhold the last three holes before reveal, while group scoring access can see its own full scores.

Read back the existing workbook and approve what can be exposed before changing Site access. Include old events and public results, roster fields and free-text correction reasons visible through group history. The observed synthetic roster does not establish that every historical row is safe to disclose. Preserve the Stage 5 and Stage 6 evidence and locked events. The public package excludes the diagnostic pages and worker, so the initial audience change cannot expose those paths through the new version. Version 16 retains its diagnostic assets for private rollback.

Use one designated scorer and one prepared device per group. Agree who monitors Apps Script timeouts and who can stop scoring. Escalate unexpected revisions, wrong-group data, unavailable local saving, or use inconsistent with the paper card. If the owner cannot accept direct anonymous reads and scoring, hold the public trial. Obtain separate approval for the Saturday cohort and any member data to be used on this endpoint.
