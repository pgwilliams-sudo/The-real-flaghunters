Record exact frontend commit, private origin/canonical root route, backend version, device model, iOS/iPadOS and Safari versions, date/time, storage/persistence result, active/waiting worker URLs and cache asset URLs. ## Required physical cases

1. **Cold offline entry:** reload offline, close the tab, reopen the canonical app URL, then terminate/reopen Safari and repeat. Choose **Reopen prepared round … group …**. Verify course, handicap snapshot, own accepted scores, BLOB and corrections/reasons. Unopened groups must require online preparation. New or expired access must not be invented offline. Public leaderboard/scorecards/history/competitions must not display private group snapshots as public results; online-only failures must be clear.
2. **Durability and lock/resume:** save numeric and BLOB scores offline and verify “saved on this device / awaiting server acceptance.” Record operation IDs and payload fingerprints. Lock/unlock the device repeatedly, including after at least five minutes, background Safari, reload, close/reopen the tab and resume. Verify retained IDs/reasons, no server-acceptance claim, unsaved-selection warning and pending export. No background upload is required or promised.
3. **Scope/privacy:** reopen group B and an older round, then reconnect with a rotated PIN. Confirm snapshots/outboxes remain separated by backend, event, group and credential. Old credential failures require renewed access and explicit review. Verify hidden holes 16–18 remain unavailable in public scorecards/rankings before reveal; own-group scoring still permits them. Offline caching must never expose other groups' private scores or cached admin credentials.
4. **Reconnect and ambiguous timeout:** reconnect, synchronize and compare immutable history with expected operation IDs. Interrupt an upload response after the backend accepts it, close/reopen Safari and retry. Confirm status reconciliation produces one accepted write/history chain, with no duplicate correction. For a real timeout capture elapsed time, action, sanitized HTTP status/response shape and backend execution timing. Retain the pending export. Do not infer rejection from timeout or raise time limits to hide it.
5. **Updates with pending work:** save pending offline operations, reconnect under controlled response loss and deliver a separately approved test update with a new app cache version. The update must wait while an old app tab remains open; no forced reload or activation. Close every app tab, reopen, and verify pending IDs/payloads/sessions/known locks and hidden-hole privacy survive. Verify diagnostic registration/cache/marker remain intact. An asset failure or sign-in redirect must fail preparation without publishing a partial shell.
6. **Known permanent lock:** observe server reveal/lock in a prepared group, then reopen offline. Scoring and Finish remain disabled. All local messages agree that results are permanently locked, no resubmission is allowed and pending requests remain exportable. A device which has not learned the lock may queue offline selections but must explain stale data; reconnection must reject changes without overwriting server scores.
7. **Storage loss/full:** use a disposable synthetic browser profile only. Simulate missing IndexedDB with shell cache retained, missing shell cache with IndexedDB retained, full/quota-denied storage and full site eviction. Verify explicit missing/unavailable-storage messages, no false durable-save or acceptance claim and no deletion of surviving pending data. A fully evicted offline origin cannot render its own warning; document Safari's load error, reconnect and verify the missing-preparation warning. Do not clear original-device data to test this.
8. **Stage 4 release blockers:** repeat selected BLOB recovery with the in-page confirmation, Back to review, double clicks, a second-device revision change and response loss. Repeat revision-history open/close/retry across lock/resume and fresh tabs. Record any blank/unresponsive page or malformed response with sanitized evidence. Stop the affected path, preserve tabs/exports, and keep S4-B1/S4-B2 open until the root cause or reproducible exclusion and regression evidence are reviewed. Provisional Stage 4 acceptance alone does not close them.

## Decision record

For every case record pass/fail, exact steps, device, time, operation IDs (no credentials), expected/actual history counts, retained exports and screenshot references. Mark actual device-lock/OS termination evidence separately from automated visibility-event tests. Any lost pending operation, cross-scope disclosure, premature acceptance, hidden-hole disclosure, damaged diagnostic worker or blank-page/history recurrence blocks acceptance. Record storage eviction limitations explicitly. Report results for review; Stage 5 deployment, production promotion, main merge and Stage 6 each require further authorization.
